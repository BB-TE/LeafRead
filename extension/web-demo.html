A quieter way to read

直接将鼠标停在下面任意英文单词上约 1 秒。不需要导入这篇文章；这页使用普通网页文字，保留原始排版。安装扩展后，普通网站也能这样查词。

After a difficult week, Maya felt exhausted. She opened an article in her browser and began reading. The first sentence was familiar, but the next paragraph contained a word she had never seen before: serendipity.

Instead of leaving the page, she moved the cursor over the unfamiliar word. A small card appeared beside it. She read the meaning, saved the word with its original sentence, and continued her journey through the story.

A small habit, a different experience

Good tools protect your attention. They let you stay curious without turning every unfamiliar sentence into a separate task. A quiet moment of discovery can be enough to make you want to read another chapter.

Web pages often use extraordinary formatting. A word may even be split across inline elements, like exhausted. The reader should recognize the complete word while preserving the author's layout.

You can select a short phrase, such as in the end, to look it up as a whole. You can also read words in a link to the next chapter.

An online book, inside a frame

The extension works with ordinary text on article pages and many online book readers. Images, scanned pages, and books rendered only on a canvas require a different method.
